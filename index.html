Our Story

Rooted in Kashmir

Shah Bakery & Fast Food was born from a simple belief — that the finest food comes from honest ingredients, time-honoured recipes, and a kitchen that cares deeply about every plate it sends out.

Nestled in the heart of Kashmir, we set out to preserve the royal tradition of Wazwan — the magnificent 36-course Kashmiri feast prepared by master chefs called Wazas — while also celebrating the vibrant street food culture of North India. Every dish on our menu carries a memory: the wood smoke of a grandmother's kitchen, the golden saffron fields of Pampore, the sizzle of a live tandoor at dawn.

We do not cook fast — we cook right. Our spices are freshly ground, our marinades work overnight, and our tandoor never goes cold. From a perfectly layered biryani to a soul-warming bowl of Gushtaba, every bite is our promise of authenticity.
